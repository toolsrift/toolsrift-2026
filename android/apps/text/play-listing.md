## Full description (≤4000 chars)

ToolsRift Text Tools puts 66 free text tools in your pocket — type, transform, refine.

Counters, converters, cleaners and generators for anyone who works with words — writers, students, editors and developers.

EVERYTHING RUNS ON YOUR DEVICE
No uploads, no accounts, no limits. Your files and text are processed locally, so nothing you work on ever leaves your phone. Most tools work fully offline once loaded.

POPULAR TOOLS
• Word Counter Pro — Count words, chars, sentences & get reading time
• Character Counter — Detailed character breakdown with limits checker
• Reading Time Calculator — Estimate reading & speaking time for any content
• Word Frequency Analyzer — Rank words by frequency with percentage breakdown
• Text Statistics — 20+ text metrics in one comprehensive report
• Unique Word Extractor — Extract all unique words from a block of text
• Palindrome Checker — Check if text, words or phrases are palindromes
• Anagram Checker — Check if two words or phrases are anagrams
• Count Occurrences — Count how many times a word or phrase appears in text
• Duplicate Word Finder — Find words that repeat, ranked by how often they occur
• Text Reverser — Reverse characters or words in any text
• Line Sorter — Sort lines alphabetically, reverse, or randomly
…and 54 more.

WHY TOOLSRIFT TEXT
✓ 100% free, forever
✓ No sign-up, no email, no tracking of your content
✓ Instant results — no waiting for a server
✓ Clean, ad-supported, no paywalls
✓ Part of the ToolsRift network of 29 specialist tool sites

ToolsRift Text is the text edition of ToolsRift (toolsrift.com), the free online tools platform. The app is a lightweight wrapper around text.toolsrift.com, so every improvement to the website ships to the app automatically.
