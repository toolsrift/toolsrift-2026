## Full description (≤4000 chars)

ToolsRift Study Tools puts 30 free study & education tools in your pocket — learn faster, free.

Flashcards, quizzes, citation generators, periodic table, Pomodoro timers and revision planners — free for every student.

EVERYTHING RUNS ON YOUR DEVICE
No uploads, no accounts, no limits. Your files and text are processed locally, so nothing you work on ever leaves your phone. Most tools work fully offline once loaded.

POPULAR TOOLS
• Flashcard Maker — Paste term and definition lines to build flippable flashcards you can shuffle, mark known or unknown, track progress on and print.
• Quiz Generator — Turn a list of questions and answers into a multiple-choice quiz you can take on screen, with instant scoring and a review of every answer.
• Multiple Choice Maker — Build a printable multiple-choice worksheet from your own questions and options, complete with a separate answer key for marking.
• Study Timer (Pomodoro) — A configurable Pomodoro focus timer with custom work, short break and long break lengths, session counting and an end-of-cycle chime.
• Spaced Repetition Planner — Enter the day you first learned a topic and get the full spaced repetition review schedule at 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days with exact dates.
• Revision Timetable Planner — Split your subjects across the days you have left before an exam and get a balanced, printable revision timetable with daily study slots.
• Weighted Grade Calculator — Enter each assessment score and its weight to work out your weighted average, current percentage and letter grade for the whole course.
• Final Grade Calculator — Work out exactly what mark you need on the final exam to reach your target overall grade, based on your current grade and the exam weight.
• GPA Planner — Find the GPA you need across your remaining credits to reach a target cumulative GPA, based on your current GPA and credits already earned.
• Attendance Percentage Calculator — Work out your current attendance percentage and how many more classes in a row you must attend to reach your required minimum.
• Exam Countdown — List your exams and see how many days are left until each one, sorted by date, with weekdays and your available study days broken down.
• Random Question Picker — Paste a question bank and draw questions at random for cold calling, self-testing or revision drills, with or without repeats.
…and 18 more.
